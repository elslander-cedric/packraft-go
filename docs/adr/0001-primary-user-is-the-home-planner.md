# 1. Primary user is the at-home trip planner

Date: 2026-10-10

## Status
Accepted

## Context
A packrafting app could serve two very different moments: planning a trip at home days or weeks ahead, or navigating while already out on the route. They need different things (a big screen and rich data vs. offline maps, GPS and battery-friendliness).

## Decision
The primary user is someone planning a multi-day trip at home, ahead of time, linking hike legs and paddle legs into one route.

## Consequences
- Design for a large screen and deliberate, unhurried use.
- In-the-field navigation is out of scope for now; the plan must be exportable to tools people already use outdoors.
