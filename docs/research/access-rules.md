# Paddling access rules in Belgium

Researched 2026-10-12. Not legal advice. The app still shows "check local navigation rules" (ADR 0010).

**Summary**
- **Flanders:** paddling is allowed by default on **navigable** waterways, with a short list of exceptions (some canals, the ports) and a ban on entering locks. It's also allowed by default on **non-navigable** rivers (BVR of 7 May 2021, art. 21), unless the river's manager bans it. No permit is needed. Details: [flanders.md](flanders.md).
- **Wallonia:** paddling is allowed **only** on rivers listed in the law (a whitelist). Each stretch has a season, permitted hours and minimum/maximum flow thresholds. You may only get in and out at designated points. The daily status is published on kayak.spw.wallonie.be.
- **Packrafts:** treated like kayaks in both regions.

## Flanders

### Navigable waterways (managed by De Vlaamse Waterweg)

Official reference: De Vlaamse Waterweg's Visuris "Kano" page, [visuris.be/kano](https://www.visuris.be/kano). The page is undated; it was read on 2026-10-12. Peddelsport Vlaanderen points to it as the authoritative source ([regelgeving op het water](https://www.peddelsport.vlaanderen/waar-het-water-op/regelgeving-op-het-water)).

- **General rule:** canoeing, kayaking, rowing and SUP are allowed on navigable inland waters, except:
  - the Ghent–Terneuzen canal
  - the fairway of the Lower Sea Scheldt (Beneden-Zeeschelde), downstream of Burcht
  - certain inner arms of waterways, for example the Reien in Bruges and the Afleiding van de Nete in Lier
- **Ports (off-limits without permission), from the port police regulations** (details and sources in [flanders.md](flanders.md)):
  - **Antwerp:** the port police regulation of 2 October 2021, art. 6.2, forbids watersport, including canoeing and kayaking, without the harbour master's permission. **[UNVERIFIED]:** whether a newer merged Port of Antwerp-Bruges version replaces it.
  - **Ghent (North Sea Port):** the port police regulation valid from 1 January 2026, art. 3.1.9, forbids water recreation in the docks without written permission.
  - **Zeebrugge:** the City of Bruges says paddling on your own initiative isn't allowed in the port. **[UNVERIFIED]:** the status of the Boudewijnkanaal itself.
- **Locks:** paddlers may not enter locks. De Vlaamse Waterweg can make exceptions, mainly for organised tours or events, and then a life vest is required. In practice you portage around every lock. **[UNVERIFIED]:** whether official landing points exist at locks.
- **Movable bridges:** opened for paddlers only when passing along a route without enough clearance, and not if landing places are available.
- **High-speed zones** (snelvaartvakken): paddling in them is treated as forbidden unless local arrangements say otherwise. The list is at [visuris.be/snelvaartvakken](https://www.visuris.be/snelvaartvakken).
- **Preferred waters:** De Vlaamse Waterweg asks paddlers to prefer small waterways, CEMT class I–II.
- **Rafts:** banned on several waterways, such as the Demer, Dijle, Zeekanaal Brussel–Schelde and Albertkanaal.
- **Permits:** none needed for kayak, canoe, rowing or SUP. Required on-board equipment: [mobilit.belgium.be](https://mobilit.belgium.be/nl/scheepvaart/pleziervaart/vaartuig/uitrusting).
- **SUP only:** no SUP between sunset and sunrise, and a leash is mandatory.
- **Seasons and water levels:** no general restrictions on navigable waterways. There are ad-hoc warnings, for example blue-green algae ([vlaamsewaterweg.be](https://www.vlaamsewaterweg.be/nieuws/blauwalgen-actueel)), and drought measures per province ([vlaanderen.be](https://www.vlaanderen.be/droogtemaatregelen/droogtemaatregelen-in-provincie-limburg)).
- **Legal basis:** the Algemeen Politiereglement voor de Scheepvaart op de Binnenwateren (APR), a royal decree of 24 September 2006, in force since 1 January 2007 and amended several times (most recently by a Flemish order in force 31 December 2022), plus special regulations per waterway. A paddler counts as a small vessel propelled by muscle power.
  - **Art. 1.06** is only a general rule not to endanger shipping.
  - **Rules for paddlers** are in art. 6.02, 6.03/1 and 6.04 (keep clear of large vessels and give way), and 3.13 §6 (a white all-round light at night).
  - **The lock ban** comes from De Vlaamse Waterweg's rules, not the APR.

### Non-navigable rivers

- **Allowed by default.** The Flemish government order (BVR) of 7 May 2021 on non-navigable watercourses (in force 8 July 2021), art. 21, says non-motorised craft may always use them ([Codex](https://codex.vlaanderen.be/PrintDocument.ashx?id=1035746&geannoteerd=false&print=false)). Older provincial and general police regulations were repealed.
- **Exceptions:** the river's manager can ban paddling, permanently or seasonally, for reasons such as nature conservation or fishing. There's **no central list** of bans and no duty to signpost them. **[UNVERIFIED]:** whether any bans currently exist.
- **Paddlers' duties:** don't disturb the peace, don't damage the banks, bed or vegetation, and don't deliberately disturb wildlife.
- **Banks and portaging [UNVERIFIED]:** rules on stepping onto private banks weren't confirmed. Some agreements forbid stepping onto the bed or banks except to get past an obstacle or in an emergency.
- **Manager by category** (the open [VHA Waterlopen WFS](https://geo.api.vlaanderen.be/VHAWaterlopen/wfs) gives the category of every segment):

| Category | Manager |
|---|---|
| 1st | Vlaamse Milieumaatschappij (VMM) |
| 2nd | the provinces |
| 3rd | municipalities, or polders and wateringen |

- **VMM paddling stretches** with agreements: Zenne (Lot–Drogenbos; **[UNVERIFIED]** whether the 2023 covenant was renewed), Dijle, Kleine Nete, and Kanaal van Stekene + Zuidlede ([VMM](https://vmm.vlaanderen.be/beleid/waterbeleid/waterrecreatie/kano-en-kajaktrajecten)). These stretches add rules on top of the default:
  - **Dijle:**
    - get in only at the 11 marked points between Huldenberg and Rotselaar
    - paddle only from sunrise to half an hour after sunset
    - don't paddle at very high or very low water
  - **Kleine Nete:**
    - 25 km from the Retie watermill to the Grobbendonk watermill, with 9 put-ins
    - no paddling in the dark
    - closed at high water at the Retie, Herentals or Grobbendonk gauges
    - closed **below 50 cm at Retie**

## Wallonia

### Legal basis

The **Arrêté du Gouvernement wallon (AGW) of 19 March 2009**, "réglementant la circulation sur et dans les cours d'eau". It's a government order (some sources call it a decree). In force since 1 July 2009; last amended 8 July 2021; consolidated version valid from 1 September 2021.

- Official text: [Wallex PDF](https://wallex.wallonie.be/files/pdfs/5/6718_Arr%C3%AAt%C3%A9_du_Gouvernement_wallon_r%C3%A9glementant_la_circulation_sur_et_dans_les_cours_d%27eau_01-09-2021-.pdf)
- Overview: [environnement.wallonie.be: textes législatifs](https://environnement.wallonie.be/home/loisirs/circulation-sur-les-cours-deau/les-textes-legislatifs.html)

### Rules

- **Scope (art. 1):** all non-navigable watercourses, plus the navigable ones listed in Annex 1.A.
- **Allowed craft (art. 2 §2):** non-motorised kayaks and canoes for up to 3 people; inflatable boats for up to 10 people; rafts (youth organisations only).
- **Where (art. 3):** only rivers listed in **Annex 1**, which acts as a whitelist.

| Category | Type | When open |
|---|---|---|
| 1.A | navigable | all year |
| 1.B | non-navigable | all year, except the two fishing opening days |
| 1.C | non-navigable | 1 October to 15 March only, except the fishing opening days |

  The fishing opening days are the 3rd Saturday of March (trout) and the 1st Saturday of June (general) (art. 4 §1).
- **Hours (art. 4 §2):**

| Category | Period | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1.B, 1.C | 1 Oct – 15 Jun | 10:00–17:00 |
| 1.B, 1.C | 16 Jun – 30 Sep | 9:30–18:00 |
| 1.A | 16 Mar – 15 Jun | 9:30–19:00 |
| 1.A | 16 Jun – 15 Oct | 9:30–20:00 |
| 1.A | 16 Oct – 15 Mar | 9:30–17:00 |

- **Getting in and out (art. 5):** only at designated **aires** for embarking and disembarking. These are set by the SPW (Service public de Wallonie) and only where the bank is freely accessible.
- **Flow conditions (art. 6):** a stretch is closed for the day if:
  - the 72-hour mean flow is below the **minimum** in Annex 2 (to protect nature), or
  - the 08:00 forecast or the observed hourly flow is above the **maximum**.

  The status is published on [kayak.spw.wallonie.be](https://kayak.spw.wallonie.be/), by phone, and on signs at each aire.
- **Example thresholds** (minimum / maximum flow):

| River (gauge) | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Amblève (Stavelot) | 1.0 m³/s | 21 m³/s |
| Ourthe (Durbuy) | 1.9 m³/s | 50 m³/s |
| Semois (Membre) | 2.2 m³/s | 50 m³/s |

  **[UNVERIFIED]:** the full Annex 2 table wasn't opened.
- **Temporary closures and exceptions (art. 7–8):** the minister can close stretches (for nature or works) or grant exceptions.
- **Practical rules** ([wallonie.be, updated 23/07/2026](https://www.wallonie.be/fr/demarches/sinformer-sur-la-pratique-du-canoe-kayak-en-wallonie)):
  - about 450 km of river open in total
  - "touristic" stretches on the Amblève, Ourthe, Lesse, Semois and Viroin
  - "sporting" stretches open in autumn and winter only, such as the upper reaches, Our, Sûre, Vierre, Aisne and Salm
  - no entering mill races
  - no bivouacking, fires or litter
  - contact: info.kayak@spw.wallonie.be
- **Rental vs private boats:** the AGW makes no distinction. Peddelsport mentions some "derogation rivers" that are open only to federation members, with prior registration. **[UNVERIFIED]:** which rivers these are.

### What kayak.spw.wallonie.be shows

- **Map:** a map of stretches, each with a status: kayaks and rafts allowed / kayaks only / ban coming soon / banned / always banned / navigable waterway.
- **Stretch details:** river, gauge, hourly and 72-hour flow, thresholds, the reason for any ban (low flow, high flow, or the 1.C season), and route notes.
- **Updates:** automatically every day at 08:15, plus manual alerts from the administration. Available in FR, NL, EN and DE.

## Packrafts

- **Flanders:** inflatable kayaks and canoes are explicitly "small vessels propelled by muscle power", the same as hard-shell boats ([visuris.be/kano](https://www.visuris.be/kano)). A packraft follows the kayak rules.
- **Wallonia:** a stricter flow rule (flow must be at least **5×** the minimum, art. 6 §4) applies only to inflatables built for **4–10 people**. A 1–2 person packraft is either a small inflatable or a kayak/canoe, and either way the normal rules apply. This is our reading of the text, not an official statement.
  - **[UNVERIFIED]:** whether kayak.spw.wallonie.be's "kayaks only" status excludes packrafts. Its "rafts" probably means large inflatables. Ask the SPW.
