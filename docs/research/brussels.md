# Brussels-Capital Region: paddling access rules (research notes)

Researched 2026-10-12. About 15 lookups. Anything marked **[UNVERIFIED]** comes from search snippets or inference, or has no primary source. Quotes are kept short.

## TL;DR for the planner
- **Canal (Charleroi–Brussels stretch + Brussels–Scheldt / Willebroek stretch inside the region) = "port domain" of the Port of Brussels.** Paddling is **allowed in principle**. Rowing craft and "other small craft" are explicitly allowed, and nautical recreation is allowed but "non prioritaires" vs commercial shipping (Regional police regulation, AGRBC 4 March 2010, art. 127–128). Source: https://etaamb.openjustice.be/fr/arrete-du-gouvernement-de-la-region-de-bruxellescapit_n2010031142.html (official: https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi/article_body.pl?language=fr&caller=summary&pub_date=10-03-16&numac=2010031142)
- **Locks (Anderlecht, Molenbeek): no rule found for small craft. Plan a portage.** **[UNVERIFIED]**
- **Senne/Zenne in Brussels: treat as not paddleable.** It runs mostly in culverts and sewer infrastructure. The only known descents were organised actions launched from a Vivaqua site. Model it as `forbidden`/`impassable`.
- **Ponds and small rivers (Woluwe, Molenbeek, Étangs d'Ixelles, Bois de la Cambre): no boats without authorisation.** Model them as `forbidden`.

## 1. Canal and port of Brussels

### Legal regime
- **Brussels Regional police regulation for the canal and port.** Adopted by the Brussels Government decree (AGRBC) of 4 March 2010 and published in the MB on 16 March 2010 (numac 2010031142). Its legal basis is the Ordinance of 3 December 1992 on the operation and development of the canal and port, art. 16bis §2 (added in 2003). The text seen was consolidated to 28‑06‑2013. Later amendments: **[UNVERIFIED]**. https://etaamb.openjustice.be/fr/arrete-du-gouvernement-de-la-region-de-bruxellescapit_n2010031142.html
- **Scope.** It applies to the "domaine portuaire" (art. 1, art. 6): the "plans d'eau, routes, quais … ponts" within the perimeter set in the 1993 cahier des charges. The text has no map. Same source.
- **History.** It replaced the federal AR of 18 August 1975 (police and navigation regulation for the canal Brussels–Rupel and the port of Brussels). The general federal inland regulation (RGP) did not apply to that canal. **[UNVERIFIED]**: the RGP carve-out comes from a search snippet only. https://etaamb.openjustice.be/fr/arrete-du-gouvernement-de-la-region-de-bruxellescapit_n2010031142.html

### What it says about paddlers
- **Art. 127.** The presence and use of sailing, rowing or motor boats, and other small craft, is allowed in the port domain. **Jet skis, sailboards and kite-type craft** need prior written authorisation from the Capitainerie. A kayak, canoe or packraft is not named, but it falls under "rowing / small craft". **[UNVERIFIED]**: no source explicitly classifies SUP or packraft.
- **Art. 128 §2.** "Les activités récréatives à caractère nautique sont autorisées", but they are "pas prioritaires par rapport à la navigation commerciale". Notices to skippers can **reserve zones and periods** for recreation. No current notice was found: **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Art. 38.** Organising a **recreational or sporting event** in the port domain needs prior authorisation from the port company. This applies to group trips and events, not individual paddlers.
- **Art. 45–46.** Every boat must carry a working VHF ("mariphone") and report to the Capitainerie when it enters, leaves, moors or casts off. The decree defines "bateau" to include small craft (art. 4 §1). Read literally, this would also cover a kayak. Whether it is enforced against paddlers is **[UNVERIFIED]**, and probably not. Flag it, and recommend paddlers call the Capitainerie.
- **Art. 83.** Béco basin: keep 50 m clear on each side of the two bridges (this is a mooring rule).
- **Not covered by the text:** small craft passing locks, life jackets, swimming, navigation hours, and keeping to the side.
- **Swimming is not allowed in the canal.** In 2025 the Capitainerie said the regional police regulation still forbids it (no Big Jump). https://www.lavenir.net/regions/bruxelles/2025/07/13/pas-de-big-jump-dans-le-canal-a-bruxelles-malgre-une-qualite-de-leau-prometteuse-la-capitainerie-du-port-estime-que-la-baignade-nest-pas-possible-BZ2WBVBZ4FBOLAR5R3SDXTDTZY/

### Locks, bridges and contacts
- **Port infrastructure.** The port operates two locks, **Anderlecht** (Charleroi–Brussels side) and **Molenbeek**, plus three mobile bridges (Buda, Gosselies, Hospices). https://be.brussels/fr/propos-de-la-region/structure-et-organisation/administrations-et-institutions-de-la-region/port-de-bruxelles (search snippet)
- **Pleasure craft at locks.** In a heatwave the Capitainerie made pleasure boats lock through in groups or with a commercial vessel, because they are "not a priority". https://bx1.be/communes/bruxelles-ville/le-port-de-bruxelles-regule-le-passage-des-bateaux-de-plaisance-en-raison-de-la-canicule/ (search snippet)
- **Flood role.** The Anderlecht lock helps prevent flooding of the city centre, so it can close or be restricted at high water. https://bx1.be/categories/news/alerte-de-crue-comment-gere-t-on-le-niveau-des-cours-deau-a-bruxelles/embed/ (search snippet)
- **Contacts.** Capitainerie: +32 2 247 91 21, Avenue du Port / Voorhavenstraat 2. VHF channel 68, given for the cruise terminal area. https://www.port.brussels/sites/default/files/2024-03/Brochure_BCT_2024_FR_0.pdf (search snippet). General port line: +32 2 420 67 00. https://port.brussels/en/news/sporting-activities
- **Portaging.** No official landing points or portage routes were found for either lock: **[UNVERIFIED]**. Assume a portage over the quays. Quays are part of the port domain, so damaging them is forbidden (art. 111). Landing elsewhere on port quays may be restricted by the private concessions there: **[UNVERIFIED]**.

### Clubs and designated activity
- **Port of Brussels leisure page.** It lists **CRBK – Cercle des Régates de Bruxelles Kayak** (Rue des Grives 53, 1070 Anderlecht, left bank, weekends), **Canal It Up** (kayak litter-picking, Béco basin, right bank), **140ste FOS Seascouts Roodbaard** (Avant-Port, BRYC nautical centre, Quai de Heembeek 4) and the **Brussels Royal Yacht Club** (Avant-Port). https://port.brussels/en/leisure/practical-information
- **Canal clubs.** The port says "many rowing, sailing and canoeing clubs" use the canal, including the Union Nautique de Bruxelles (rowing). https://port.brussels/en/news/sporting-activities
- **Port policy.** The port's management contract for 2021–2025 includes water-side leisure and sport. https://www.port.brussels/sites/default/files/2021-12/HVB_Beheerscontract_LOW.pdf (search snippet)

### Outside the region
- North of the regional border, the Zeekanaal Brussel–Schelde is managed by De Vlaamse Waterweg. South of the border, the Charleroi–Brussels canal is managed by De Vlaamse Waterweg on the Flemish Brabant stretch (Halle) and by SPW in Wallonia **[UNVERIFIED]**. These stretches are covered in `flanders.md` and `wallonia.md` (Flemish raft ban on the Zeekanaal; lock entry banned). Nothing new was researched here.

## 2. Senne/Zenne and other Brussels waters

- **Senne.** About 14 km cross the region, mostly underground. It is open only on short stretches near the Anderlecht lock (south) and near the treatment plant (north). In 2020 and 2022 Canal It Up paddled the vaulted section, launching through a hatch at a **Vivaqua** site behind Gare du Midi. These were organised actions, and the 2020 trip was reportedly done with Vivaqua's agreement (**[UNVERIFIED]**: search snippet). https://bx1.be/categories/news/une-descente-en-kayak-pas-comme-les-autres-sur-la-senne-souterraine/ ; https://www.dhnet.be/regions/bruxelles/ils-parcourent-la-senne-souterraine-en-kayak-elle-n-est-pas-en-bonne-sante-6296fb5c7b50a6292b81005c
  - No explicit navigation ban was found. Public access to the culverts is not possible without the operator, though. **Planner: impassable / forbidden.** **[UNVERIFIED]**: no legal text was found.
  - The legal paddling route nearest Brussels is the Flemish Zenne route from Lot to Drogenbos, 6.3 km. It ends just outside the region. https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2023/06/25/iedereen-kan-voortaan-kajakken-op-de-zenne-in-lot-net-zo-avont/ (already in `flanders.md`)
- **Non-navigable watercourses and ponds** (Senne, Woluwe, Molenbeek, Neerpedebeek, Hollebeek-Leibeek, Geleytsbeek, …). They are governed by the **Ordinance of 16 May 2019** on the management and protection of non-navigable watercourses and ponds, in force since 1 January 2020. The **AGRBC of 23 May 2024** sits under it: it classifies the watercourses, designates about 50 regional ponds and adopts the Atlas of the hydrographic network. The **canal is excluded**. The page does not mention navigation. It does prohibit encroaching on a classified watercourse or pond ("empiéter"). https://environnement.brussels/citoyen/reglementation-et-inspection/obligations-et-autorisations/la-gestion-et-la-protection-des-cours-deau-non-navigables-et-des-etangs-bruxellois ; ordinance: https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/ordonnance/2019/05/16/2019012903/justel
  - Unlike Flanders, Brussels law gives **no default right to paddle** non-navigable water: **[UNVERIFIED]**. None was found. These streams are also tiny and partly culverted.
- **Ponds.** Bruxelles Environnement says that "sauf autorisation, il n'est pas permis d'utiliser une embarcation sur les étangs bruxellois" (bx1, 7 May 2020). The **Bois de la Cambre** lake is managed by the **City of Brussels**, not the Region. https://bx1.be/communes/bruxelles-ville/un-kayakiste-qui-demandait-la-regularisation-de-sans-papier-arrete-au-bois-de-la-cambre/
  - Swimming is forbidden in ponds and fountains managed by Bruxelles Environnement. https://www.lalibre.be/regions/bruxelles/2020/08/07/meme-en-pleine-canicule-fontaines-et-etangs-restent-interdits-dacces-a-bruxelles-G6LI37TICBFK5IJATJ7NHPZV5A/
  - The Bois de la Cambre lake is described as a "boating lake" and posts botulism warnings in hot summers. Whether a boat-hire concession operates there is **[UNVERIFIED]**. https://www.rtbf.be/article/danger-botulisme-des-oiseaux-morts-decouverts-au-bois-de-la-cambre-a-bruxelles-11421506 (search snippet). Own craft: **not allowed** without authorisation, per the bx1 quote above, though that quote concerns the regional ponds generally. **[UNVERIFIED]** for City-managed ponds specifically, which are governed by the City's police regulation (not found).
  - **Étangs d'Ixelles:** no specific rule found. The ponds rule above applies by default: **[UNVERIFIED]**.

## 5. Packraft and practical notes

- **Craft type.** The port regulation has no packraft, kayak or SUP category. These craft fit "rowing / small craft" (art. 127), so no authorisation is needed. Only jet skis, sailboards and kite craft need one. Interpreting SUP this way is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Commercial traffic has priority (art. 128 §2).** The canal carries barges and the sea-port traffic of the Avant-Port, so keep to the edge. The art. 45 VHF and reporting rule technically covers every "bateau". Advise a courtesy call to the Capitainerie (+32 2 247 91 21) before a transit: **[UNVERIFIED]** whether it is required in practice.
- **Locks.** No rule allows small craft into the Anderlecht or Molenbeek locks, and Flanders bans paddlers from locks outright. **Planner: portage at both locks.** Exits and landing points: **[UNVERIFIED]**.
- **Water quality.** Swimming is banned and the Senne is polluted (combined-sewer overflows), so avoid capsizing and immersion.
- **Events and groups:** prior authorisation from the port (art. 38).
- **Flood management:** the Anderlecht lock can be closed or restricted at high water.

## Suggested `access-rules.md` entry (draft)
- **Brussels-Capital Region:** paddling is **allowed** on the canal and port basins (port domain). It follows the regional police regulation of 4 March 2010 (art. 127–128), with commercial traffic taking priority and the port able to reserve zones. Portage around the Anderlecht and Molenbeek locks. **Not allowed** on regional ponds without authorisation. The Senne is culverted and impassable. Small streams have no default access right **[UNVERIFIED]**.
