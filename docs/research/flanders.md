# Flanders paddling access: research findings

Researched 2026-10-12. Detailed findings behind the Flanders section of [access-rules.md](access-rules.md). Quotes are under 15 words. **[UNVERIFIED]** marks anything not confirmed in a primary source. "BVR" means Besluit van de Vlaamse Regering (Flemish government order).

---

## 1. Non-navigable watercourses: **allowed by default** (for non-motorised craft)

### Legal basis (confirmed)

The **BVR of 7 May 2021** implementing the law of 28 December 1967 on non-navigable watercourses. Published in the Belgisch Staatsblad on 28 June 2021 and in force from **8 July 2021**.

- Codex Vlaanderen: https://codex.vlaanderen.be/PrintDocument.ashx?id=1035746&geannoteerd=false&print=false
- Staatsblad PDF: https://emis.vito.be/sites/emis/files/legislation/91/2021/sb280621-5.pdf
- Entry into force on 8 July 2021, per Rechtenkrant: https://rechtenkrant.be/er-komt-een-uniforme-regeling-voor-alle-onbevaarbare-waterlopen-in-vlaanderen/

**Art. 21, Afdeling 6 ("varen")** (Codex link above):

- **Motorised boats:** banned without the water manager's written permission. The text says "Varen met gemotoriseerde vaartuigen is verboden, tenzij na een schriftelijke toestemming".
- **Non-motorised craft:** "De onbevaarbare waterlopen zijn altijd bevaarbaar voor niet-gemotoriseerde vaartuigen". The water manager (waterbeheerder) can restrict this permanently or for part of the year on all or part of a watercourse, for five reasons:
  1. the goals of integrated water policy
  2. nature conservation
  3. quiet use of fishing rights or other recreation
  4. maintenance and other works
  5. any other reason of general interest
- **Duties while paddling:** do not disturb the peace, do not damage banks, bed or vegetation, and do not deliberately disturb wild animals.
- **Liability:** the water manager is not liable for accidents.
- **Explanatory note:** restrictions must be "duidelijk gemotiveerd", for example based on nature management or species protection plans. Art. 21 itself sets **no requirement to publish or signpost** a ban.

**Old rules repealed.** Art. 43 repeals the royal decree of 5 August 1970 (the general police regulation for non-navigable watercourses) and the 1958 police regulation for polders and wateringen. The decree of 26 April 2019 (art. 24) abolishes the provincial regulations ("provinciale reglementen") for non-navigable watercourses (Codex link above). So the provincial police regulations on non-navigable watercourses no longer apply. The landelijk.vlaanderen summary says the same: https://landelijk.vlaanderen/nieuws/nieuw-besluit-betreffende-de-onbevaarbare-waterlopen/

### Verdict

**Default allowed** for kayak, canoe, packraft and SUP. It is **restricted only where the competent water manager has imposed a reasoned ban.** That manager is VMM for 1st category, the province for 2nd category, and the municipality or polder for 3rd category. The idea that rivers are "not allowed unless designated" (currently `access-rules.md` line 48) is **wrong** and should be replaced.

The VMM agreements (overeenkomsten) are management and safety arrangements on top of the default. They are not permits. For example, the Kleine Nete agreement adds bans tied to water levels and hours (see §2).

### Other layers that can still restrict access

- **Water manager bans (art. 21):** these can exist per stretch or per season, for example during the bird breeding season. **[UNVERIFIED]:** no central register of such bans was found. Each manager decides and publishes, or doesn't, in its own way. The older Kajako page (August 2020) mentions a breeding-season limit on the Zuidlede: https://kajako.weebly.com/peddelen-en-de-wetgeving.html
- **Nature reserves and Natura 2000:** art. 21 2° lets the water manager ban paddling for nature conservation. Separately, the access rules of nature reserves (toegankelijkheidsregeling, under the Natuurdecreet) may close water inside a reserve. **[UNVERIFIED]:** this was not checked against the Natuurdecreet or Natuurbesluit text. The Kleine Nete agreement notes that stretches lie in SBZ (special protection areas) and the VEN (Flemish Ecological Network). Source: the VMM PDF below.
- **Fishing:** fishing appears only as a ground for a ban (art. 21 3°). **[UNVERIFIED]:** no fishing-law provision was found that restricts paddling directly.
- **Municipal rules:** **[UNVERIFIED]:** none were found that apply in general. Local police regulations can cover specific places. For example, Bruges forbids going on the water on your own initiative on the Reitjes and part of the Ringvaart: https://www.brugge.be/vrije-tijd/sport/meer-sporten/kajakken-kanovaren-roeien-suppen
- **Riparian property, banks and portage:** **[UNVERIFIED]:** no source was found on who owns the bed, or on a general right to land or portage across private banks. The BVR is silent on landing and on walking on banks. The VMM Kleine Nete agreement forbids walking on the bed or banks except to pass a fallen tree or in an emergency, and allows getting out only at the official put-in and take-out points. Treat landing outside official points as not permitted without the owner's consent.

**Demer, Grote Nete, Zwalm, Mark, IJzer:** **[UNVERIFIED]:** no provincial or municipal paddling agreement was found. Note:

- The navigable Demer and the IJzer fall under De Vlaamse Waterweg, and the general Visuris rules apply there.

## 3. Ports

The Visuris "Havenreglementering" page only says that each seaport has its own regulations, and links to them. It gives no kayak rules: https://www.visuris.be/havenreglementering

- **Port of Antwerp:** the **Havenpolitieverordening (HPV) of 2 October 2021**: https://themis.vlaanderen.be/files/61dc5f5cd129f6000c00173a/download
  - Def. 80 defines watersport as including rowing, canoeing and kayaking.
  - **Art. 6.2:** port users may not practise watersport activities "tenzij met toelating van HKD" (unless permitted by the harbour master's office).
  - **Art. 6.4:** for pleasure craft, locks are forbidden, and passage is allowed only by the shortest route from Noordlandbrug to the Stadshaven or the Albertkanaal. A VHF radio and a certificate are required.
  - **Verdict:** forbidden without HKD permission.
  - The port area is defined as the area set by the Flemish government plus Scheldt strips (law of 19 June 1978).
  - **[UNVERIFIED]:** whether a newer merged Port of Antwerp-Bruges HPV has replaced the 2021 text. The PoAB page did not render: https://www.portofantwerpbruges.com/en/shipping/rules-and-procedures/port-regulations-and-bylaws
- **Port of Ghent (North Sea Port):** the **Havenpolitieverordening North Sea Port, Havengebied Gent**, approved by the BVR of 12-12-2025, valid from **01-01-2026**: https://media.northseaport.com/cdn/Nj5t2Sbds7C9/north-sea-port-havenpolitieverordening-gent-2026.pdf
  - **Art. 3.1.9:** "elke vorm van pleziervaart en waterrecreatie is verboden in de dokken" (all pleasure boating and water recreation is banned in the docks), except with written permission from the harbour master's office.
  - Its geographic scope is the port area drawn on the annexed plan.
  - The Ghent–Terneuzen canal is also excluded by Visuris: https://www.visuris.be/kano
  - **Verdict:** forbidden.
- **Zeebrugge and the Boudewijnkanaal:** Bruges says going on the water on your own initiative is "niet toegelaten" in the port of Zeebrugge: https://www.brugge.be/vrije-tijd/sport/meer-sporten/kajakken-kanovaren-roeien-suppen
  - The Zeebrugge regulation (for the port, the sea canal and the Bruges docks) is linked from Visuris at https://portofzeebrugge.be/nl/nautisch/e-loket but was **not read**.
  - AS Adventure lists "Kanaal Gent-Terneuzen en Zeebrugge-Brugge" as exceptions: https://www.asadventure.com/nl/expertise-tips/outdoor/kajakvaren-in-eigen-land-waar-mag-het.html
  - **Verdict:** **[UNVERIFIED], probably forbidden.** The Boudewijnkanaal specifically still needs the Zeebrugge port regulation text.

## 4. Algemeen Politiereglement (APR): dates and versions

- **Royal decree (KB) of 24 September 2006**, published in the Belgisch Staatsblad on 3 October 2006 (NUMAC 2006014132), in force 1 January 2007: https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/cgi_loi/change_lg_2.pl?language=nl&nm=2006014132&la=N
- **Amendments:**
  - KB of 20-09-2009
  - KB of 15-12-2013
  - KB of 04-04-2014
  - **BVR of 05-10-2018** (published 12-12-2018)
  - KB of 28-06-2019 (federal provisions)
  - **BVR of 25-11-2022** (published 21-12-2022, in force 31-12-2022)

  Source: the Justel link above.
- **Art. 1.06 "Gebruik van de vaarweg":** a vessel's length, width, draught, height, speed and manoeuvrability must not endanger navigation. It contains **nothing specific to paddlers**. Source: the Visuris consolidated text, dated 17-12-2018 and so older than the 2022 amendment: https://www.visuris.be/download/b71fa20b-6aaa-4abc-ac95-2c89c0f8ab59?dl=0
- **Muscle-powered craft in the APR:**
  - **art. 6.02:** small craft must leave large vessels room to keep course and manoeuvre
  - **art. 6.03/1 and 6.04:** muscle-powered craft give way to sailing craft, and motor craft give way to both
  - **art. 3.13 §6:** a lone muscle-powered small craft shows an all-round white light at night
  - **art. 6.28 §3:** a small craft enters a lock only after the large vessels
- The **ban on entering locks** is not in the APR. It sits in De Vlaamse Waterweg's own rules (Visuris kano page, "Algemene tijdelijke voorschriften": https://www.visuris.be/download/e8d5a750-ea8d-43fb-b6ca-2462f5931db7?dl=0). **[UNVERIFIED]:** this exact clause was not read.
- **Special regulations per waterway** (bijzondere reglementen, e.g. Beneden-Zeeschelde, Gent–Terneuzen): **[UNVERIFIED]:** dates not collected. Visuris indexes them at https://www.visuris.be/Scheepvaartreglementering

## 5. Open data for mapping

- **VHA Waterlopen** (watercourse axes with category and manager attributes): WFS at `https://geo.api.vlaanderen.be/VHAWaterlopen/wfs`. Metadata: https://www.geopunt.be/catalogus/webservicefolder/6aac49dd-1724-de8f-37ee-dc38-3d66-dd8d-ecfcd081
  - **Licence:** data.gov.be lists the **Open Data Licentie Vlaanderen** (https://data.gov.be/nl/dataset/7606f779-9c54-4f04-8ff2-1dc03dbed783). An older 2021 notice cites the Modellicentie Gratis Hergebruik (https://overheid.vlaanderen.be/informatie-vlaanderen/nieuws/update-vlaamse-hydrografische-atlas-28/08/2021).
  - This dataset gives us the navigable/non-navigable split and who manages each segment, which is who can impose art. 21 bans.
- **Snelvaartvakken (high-speed zones):** **[UNVERIFIED]:** no WFS or download was found. Visuris only offers an interactive map (https://www.visuris.be/pleziervaartopkaart; https://www.visuris.be/snelvaartvakken). For data, ask ris@vlaamsewaterweg.be (contact listed at https://www.visuris.be/databeheer).
- **Port boundaries:** **[UNVERIFIED]:** no open dataset was found. The Ghent HPV uses an annexed plan, and the Antwerp HPV refers to the area set by the Flemish government. A Geopunt search for "zeehavengebied" is the next step.
- **Art. 21 bans and VMM stretches:** no open geometry found. VMM publishes maps only as PDFs.
